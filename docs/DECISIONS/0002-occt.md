# ADR 0002: Geometry kernel — OCCT

## Status

Accepted for Mechanical / Fusion / Mill solid modeling (2026-10-10). Draft remains analytic 2D.

## Context

Mechanical CAD, Fusion, and CAM need a topology-aware B-rep kernel. Writing one from scratch is multi-year and error-prone. Open CASCADE Technology is available under LGPL and is installed for local builds via Homebrew (`opencascade`).

## Decision

1. **MyDesk Mechanical, Fusion, and Mill** link Open CASCADE Technology behind product-local adapters under `products/<id>/` and optional shared `core/brep` facades that do not leak into Draft public APIs.
2. **Draft** continues with lightweight analytic 2D entities and does not require OCCT at link time.
3. Feature history, sketches, and regeneration live in product document models; OCCT shapes are the geometric payload of solid features.
4. Boolean failures, invalid topology, and tolerance problems must surface as diagnostics — never silent corruption.
5. STEP import/export uses OCCT `STEPControl_*` where lawful and tested.

## Consequences

- Pin OCCT version in CI and `docs/legal/THIRD_PARTY.md`.
- Product CMake sets `CMAKE_PREFIX_PATH` to the OCCT prefix when `MYDESK_PRODUCT` needs it.
- Draft CI must not fail if OCCT is absent.
- Toy scalar “volume = w*h*d” engines are not acceptable substitutes for B-rep features.
